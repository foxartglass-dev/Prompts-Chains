Analyze these reference images and extract the complete visual style DNA.

## Extract These Elements:

### Lighting
- Type (natural, studio, mixed)
- Direction (front, side, back, overhead)
- Quality (hard, soft, diffused)
- Mood (warm, cool, neutral)

### Color
- Overall palette (warm, cool, neutral)
- Saturation level (vibrant, muted, natural)
- Key colors present
- Color grading style

### Composition
- Framing (tight, medium, wide)
- Subject placement (centered, rule of thirds, etc.)
- Depth of field (shallow, deep, mixed)
- Camera angle (eye level, low, high)

### Technical
- Apparent camera type (DSLR, phone, professional)
- Lens characteristics (wide, normal, telephoto)
- Image quality markers

### Mood/Atmosphere
- Overall feeling
- Professional vs casual
- Energy level

### Environment Type
- Location category (indoor-home, indoor-office, outdoor-yard, outdoor-commercial, on-site-field)
- Setting details
- Background characteristics
